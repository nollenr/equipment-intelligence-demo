4. Response to B17 Thermal Derating

Use this procedure when an HPS-2500X inverter remains online in a B17 derated state. Stop and use the site’s higher-severity response if the asset is faulted, offline, damaged, or presents an unsafe condition.

4.1 Preconditions

  • Confirm the event is B17 and the current state is Derated—not Thermal Trip.
  • Review the ten-minute controller-temperature and active-power summaries.
  • Obtain the required work authorization and apply all site safety controls.
  • Do not open energized compartments under this synthetic procedure.

4.2 Authorized inspection sequence

  1. Record controller-temperature peak, configured threshold, ambient-temperature peak, and current active-power output.
  2. From the exterior, inspect intake screens and exhaust openings for visible obstruction, vegetation, dust loading, or water intrusion.
  3. Review fan command and fan-feedback indications. If feedback is missing or unstable, place the inverter in maintenance hold and escalate.
  4. Confirm temperature is declining after derating. If temperature is stable or rising, do not reset; notify the control room and reliability engineering.
  5. When OEM recovery criteria are met, acknowledge B17 through the approved operator interface and monitor for ten minutes.
Do not reset solely because output is reduced. The protective state must be understood and the cooling path checked first.
