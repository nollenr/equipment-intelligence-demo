2. Fleet Pattern and Engineering Guidance

Synthetic fleet review found that B17 frequency increased when ambient temperature exceeded 35 °C and cooling-path restriction was also present. Ambient temperature alone was not a sufficient predictor.

2.1 Observed pattern

ConditionSynthetic observationInterpretation
Ambient ≤ 35 °CLow B17 frequencyNormal thermal margin in reviewed sample
Ambient > 35 °C, clear cooling pathOccasional short deratingHigh ambient reduces margin but does not prove a defect
Ambient > 35 °C plus restricted airflowRepeated or longer B17 eventsPrioritize cooling-path inspection

2.2 Engineering recommendations

  • Treat 35 °C as a fleet-analysis context threshold, not an OEM trip or derating setpoint.
  • Do not infer fan failure or contamination from temperature and weather data alone.
  • For repeated B17 events, compare affected units with same-model peers and review inspection results.
  • Retain the exact metric window, document versions, and field disposition with each diagnostic run.
For the Manatee example: A 36.8 °C ambient peak provides relevant context, while the 78.4 °C controller peak versus the 75 °C OEM threshold establishes the deterministic exceedance.
