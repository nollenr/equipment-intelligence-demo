4. Field Inspection

Use this procedure for a TRC-8 tracker row placed in Safe Stow Hold after P09. These steps authorize observation from outside the row movement envelope only.

4.1 Preconditions

  • Confirm the row identifier, P09 event time, Safe Stow Hold, and control-room work authorization.
  • Establish and maintain the marked movement exclusion zone for the full affected row.
  • Record commanded angle, measured angle, deviation, and drive-current summaries.
  • Stop for visible structural damage, loose modules, electrical hazards, unstable ground, or any condition requiring entry into the movement envelope.
Stop-work boundary: This procedure does not authorize motion commands, entry beneath or between moving members, removal of guards, or contact with the drive, linkage, torque tube, or position sensor.

4.2 Authorized tracker-row inspection

  1. Verify TRK-01 remains in Safe Stow Hold and notify the control room that observation is beginning.
  2. From outside the movement envelope, inspect the row path for vegetation, windblown debris, pooled material, and row-to-row or module-to-module interference.
  3. Observe visible modules, supports, torque-tube alignment, linkage, and drive housing for displacement, bending, loose material, or impact evidence. Do not touch components.
  4. Compare commanded and measured angle over the event window. Record whether feedback is present, stable, and responsive; also record abnormal or sustained drive-current loading.
  5. If deviation remains above 5.0 degrees, feedback is missing or unstable, drive current remains above 7.5 amperes, or the path is not visibly clear, retain Safe Stow Hold and create a qualified-maintenance work order.

Escalate to qualified maintenance when

  • Any check requires entry into the movement envelope or contact with tracker components.
  • Obstruction cannot be safely characterized from outside the exclusion zone.
  • Position disagreement or elevated loading persists after the visible path is confirmed clear.
