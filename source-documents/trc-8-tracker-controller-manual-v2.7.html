6. Position Monitoring

The TRC-8 compares the commanded row angle with measured position feedback and records a derived absolute deviation during automatic tracking.

6.1 Signal interpretation

SignalMeaningInterpretation limit
Commanded angleController-requested tracker angle.Does not prove physical row position.
Measured angleReported row position.May be affected by sensing, linkage, or drive conditions.
Position deviationAbsolute difference between command and measurement.Identifies disagreement; it does not identify a failed component.

6.2 Motion safety boundary

  • Keep the affected row in Safe Stow Hold while position disagreement remains unresolved.
  • Remain outside the tracker movement envelope and use the site-approved motion-control process.
  • Contact with drive, torque-tube, linkage, or sensor components requires qualified maintenance and the applicable isolation and lockout/tagout procedure.
Safety note: A stationary row may move unexpectedly if controls, stored energy, or an obstruction change. This synthetic manual does not replace site procedures.

6.3 Fault P09 — Tracker Position Deviation

Fault P09 is asserted when absolute commanded-versus-measured row-position deviation exceeds 5.0 degrees during the controller diagnostic window. The affected row enters Safe Stow Hold and automatic motion is inhibited pending inspection.

Expected behavior: P09 establishes that commanded and measured position disagreed beyond the configured limit. It does not, by itself, prove a failed actuator, position sensor, drive, or linkage. Obstruction, row interference, mechanical drag, feedback sensing, and control-path conditions remain possible until inspected.

Required response

  1. Confirm the asset, P09 event window, and Safe Stow Hold with the control room.
  2. Review commanded angle, measured angle, position deviation, and drive-motor current for the same window.
  3. From outside the movement envelope, inspect the visible row path and structure for vegetation, debris, row-to-row interference, displaced modules, or damaged members.
  4. Do not command motion, enter the movement envelope, or touch drive components under this diagnostic step.
  5. Use the approved site inspection procedure and return-to-automatic checklist before release.
Evidence interpretation: Elevated drive current is consistent with increased loading, but neither current nor position deviation alone establishes the underlying cause.
